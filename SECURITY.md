# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability in Secori software

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository (Security tab,
"Report a vulnerability"), or email security@secori.io. Please include the version,
what you observed, the impact you believe it has and steps to reproduce. Do not open a
public issue for a vulnerability.

We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days and to tell you what we decide. We do not
pursue good-faith research that stays within these terms.

## How Secori reports to others

We find vulnerabilities in other people's software and treat them the way we ask to be
treated. We report privately first, through the project's own channel. We give a
reasonable window to fix, 90 days by default, shorter if the issue is being exploited
and longer if the maintainer is actively working on it. We publish only after a fix
ships or the window ends, at the level of the class of problem, without exploit
detail. Every report is read and verified by a person before it is sent.
